## Personal-use scope

AIVQ Media Store is a private utility maintained by Romulo Drumond for the
owner's authorized media archive. It is not a public storage service and does
not offer accounts to the general public.

Use the app only with accounts, files, and devices you own or have permission
to use. You remain responsible for your media, the rights needed to store or
share it, and compliance with applicable laws and Google's service terms.

## Authorization and control

The app uses Google OAuth rather than collecting your Google password. Review
the requested permissions before granting access. You can revoke access
through your Google Account at any time. Revocation does not delete existing
archive files or local records; those must be managed separately.

Archiving a file does not authorize its publication or change its public
sharing permissions. Keep credentials private and verify the selected account
and files before requesting an archive, restore, or deletion.

## Availability and backups

The app is provided as a personal utility without a guaranteed service level.
Access may be interrupted by authentication changes, connectivity problems,
storage limits, or changes to Google's APIs. Integrity checks reduce risk but
do not replace independent backups; verify restored files before deleting
other copies.
