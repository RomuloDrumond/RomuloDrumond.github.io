Effective date: September 5, 2026.

This policy applies to **AIVQ Media Store**, a personal-use archive utility
maintained by Romulo Drumond. It describes the archive integration, not unrelated
applications or other services that may use the same media.

## Information accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the app accesses:

- Media files created by the app or explicitly made available to it under
  Google's `drive.file` permission.
- Associated metadata, including file and folder identifiers, file names, media
  types, sizes, checksums, ownership, and archive properties.
- The connected account's display name and email address, and its Drive storage
  quota and usage, to verify the archive account and available capacity.
- OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to perform authorized operations. The
  app does not receive or store Google account passwords.

## How information is used

Data is used to upload, organize, verify, audit, and restore the owner's selected
media and to record the results of those operations. Local records may include
checksums, media dimensions or duration, local paths, remote identifiers, and
operation receipts needed for integrity checks and recovery.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used by this archive
integration to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial-intelligence
or machine-learning models.

## Storage and disclosure

Media is stored in the owner's Google Drive and, where needed, on computers or
storage environments controlled by the owner. Archive metadata and receipts are
retained in the owner's project records. On macOS, OAuth credentials are stored
in Apple Keychain. A configured headless environment can instead use
owner-restricted credential files.

The integration sends authorized data to Google's APIs to perform the requested
operations. It does not automatically publish archived files or grant public
access. Any separate distribution or sharing of media requires the owner's
instructions and is not enabled by visiting these pages or authorizing the app.

These information pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may
process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and
secure the website; see
[GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
Archive media and OAuth credentials are not included in the public website.

## Retention, revocation, and deletion

Archived files and integrity records are retained until the owner chooses to
remove them. Revoking authorization prevents future authorized access but does
not itself delete files already stored in Drive, local copies, or archive
receipts.

The owner can revoke AIVQ Media Store's access in
[Google Account connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections), remove
stored credentials from the devices running the app, and delete archive files
and local records separately. For assistance or a deletion request, contact
[rbp.drumond@gmail.com](mailto:rbp.drumond@gmail.com). Deletion requests should
identify the relevant files or account without including passwords or tokens.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

AIVQ Media Store's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs
will adhere to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including its Limited Use requirements.

## Changes and contact

Material changes to these practices will be reflected on this page with an
updated effective date. Contact Romulo Drumond at
[rbp.drumond@gmail.com](mailto:rbp.drumond@gmail.com) with privacy questions.
